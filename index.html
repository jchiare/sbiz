The fourth trimester

The first months after birth are some of the hardest of a mother's life. She's healing from birth while caring for a newborn around the clock. You're the person closest to her, and you can make the biggest difference.

Her body is healing

Whether it was a vaginal birth or a C-section, recovery takes weeks. She needs rest, food, water, and someone else handling the rest.

She's feeding around the clock

Breast or bottle, newborns eat every few hours, day and night. There's a lot you can take on so she isn't doing it alone.

Her mind matters too

Big emotions are normal. Knowing the signs of postpartum depression and anxiety, and when to get help, is one of the most important things you can learn.

Why Into The Fourth

Pregnancy comes with appointments, classes and checklists. Then the baby arrives, and most of that support disappears just when mothers need it most.

Partners want to help but often don't know how. Into The Fourth gives them the knowledge and confidence to provide the care new mothers deserve.

If you're worried about her right now: call 911 in an emergency. In the US, the National Maternal Mental Health Hotline is free, confidential and open 24/7: call or text 1-833-TLC-MAMA (1-833-852-6262).
